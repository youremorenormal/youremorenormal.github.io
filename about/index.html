About the author

For years, Ahsan Raza believed that what he felt was his alone, and that nobody could understand a problem only one person had. He was wrong, and it took him a long time to find out how wrong. The people around him, friends, family and sometimes strangers, were what finally helped, and he had never wanted to ask.

He wrote this book for the reader who is tired and does not say so, to give you the conversation he wishes he had been able to have: plain, kind, and built on what the research really shows. His hope is that on a heavy night, someone opens these pages, finds out they are not the only one, and sleeps a little easier, and that tomorrow they say one sentence out loud to a person they trust, and that person says, me too.
