Why does my day look so grey?

You photograph the kettle, yesterday's mug and the crumbs, and the picture seems to say everything about your life.

You are not the only one

This is one of the twelve feelings almost everyone has, and almost nobody mentions. It does not mean something is wrong with you.

What Chapter 4 does with it

Chapter 4 of You’re More Normal Than You Think is called “Same Day, Different Lens”. It looks at how the same corner of a day can look different when you change how you look at it.
