Why does everyone else seem to have it figured out?

Other people look calmer and further along, and you are the one keeping up appearances. It shows up in a crowded room, or at 1 a.m. when you are reading reviews of a toaster you do not need.

You are not the only one

This is one of the twelve feelings almost everyone has, and almost nobody mentions. It does not mean something is wrong with you.

What Chapter 1 does with it

Chapter 1 of You’re More Normal Than You Think is called “Why It's Normal to Feel Not Normal”. It looks at the gap between how you feel inside and what other people show, and why almost everyone is doing the same sum about everyone else.
