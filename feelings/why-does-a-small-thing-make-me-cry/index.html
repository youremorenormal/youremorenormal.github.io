Why does a small thing make me cry?

Sometimes a small, ordinary thing opens a feeling that has been waiting for weeks.

You are not the only one

This is one of the twelve feelings almost everyone has, and almost nobody mentions. It does not mean something is wrong with you.

What Chapter 7 does with it

Chapter 7 of You’re More Normal Than You Think is called “Cry When You Can”. It looks at the feeling that waits, where you learned to look at the ceiling, and how to put it into words.
