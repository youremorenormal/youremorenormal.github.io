Do people really notice my mistakes?

There is a mark on your cuff at a party, and for two hours it is all you can think about.

You are not the only one

This is one of the twelve feelings almost everyone has, and almost nobody mentions. It does not mean something is wrong with you.

What Chapter 12 does with it

Chapter 12 of You’re More Normal Than You Think is called “No One's Watching”. It looks at why you feel lit up more brightly in your own head than anyone else sees you, and what to do with your hands in an empty room.

Do this nowTen minutes: phone face down, then write what your hands did. Every chapter ends with something small like this, so you finish it in a few minutes.
